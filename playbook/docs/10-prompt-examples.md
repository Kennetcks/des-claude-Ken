# 10 – Prompt examples that worked

Plain language is fine. Say *which file/window*, *what to change*, *what not to change*, and whether to save.

## Deswik

- "Why is this solid starting before the drive gets to it? It's not following our rules." → read-only investigation, then a one-link fix proposal.
- "Add the FS link RAD #4 → SLOT #1 on this level (lag 0). Don't level the schedule."
- "Relevel for me." (only when you want dates to move)
- "Can you apply a filter for the G390 MLA and the cuts after it, so I can take a screenshot? Make sure it's SCHEDULED = 1."
- "On the design layer centreline there's a flat section where we'll add a fan chamber – update the attributes from this GA. Don't change the gradient."

## 3MS documentation

- "Update this month's physicals in the Documentation folder, considering SCH = 1."
- "Update the presentation with the information from this Excel. Update the comments as you think they should be."
- "Slides 10–14: only month metres, next three months, no from/to dates." / "Keep bold only the heading names."
- "Review the slides and check the numbers add up – don't change anything, tell me first."
- "The Grade Bins have numbers in blank rows – check it, you updated it."

## Reporting and approvals

- "Update the 3MS numbers in the Actuals and 3MS tab from this month's 3MS. Show me before and after. Don't save."
- "Add equipment and KPI rows like the picture from the other site, same colour."
- "Update the CER with this quote – it's for the new portal, not in the budget, brought forward, cost in December."
- "Update only the values with the revised quote, nothing else."
- "Read my HRA and give me feedback."
- "Draft an email explaining the deck is a draft with a lot of uncertainty – not too formal."
- "Update the OneNote handover with what was done; put my updates in red."
