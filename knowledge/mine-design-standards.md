# Underground mine design standards (reference values)

Typical design parameters for Australian underground hard-rock mines (sublevel / longhole open stoping, truck haulage by decline), collected from the engineer's library of site guidelines. Use them as **starting defaults** when a request doesn't give a value, and say which value you used.

**Priority:** the engineer's own rules win: [decline-and-level-design.md](decline-and-level-design.md) and [stope-design.md](stope-design.md). Then the site's own standards (measure the existing designs in the drawing). Then this file.

Sources (codes used below):
- **Site A**: mine design parameters, development and stope design guidelines.
- **Site B**: development design procedure, stope design guidelines.
- **Site C**: development design procedure.
- **Site D**: mine planning Deswik user manual.
- **Site E**: development design procedure, Deswik project manual.
- **HRMH**: Hard Rock Miner's Handbook, Rules of Thumb (ed. 3). **Site F**: Site F planning and design guidelines.

Values differ by site because the fleet differs. Where sites disagree the range is given; check which trucks and loaders the mine runs.

Stripping for small items (Site A/Site B/Site D): jumbo / starter box 2.5–3 W x 3–3.5 H x 1–1.5 m deep, 0.5 m above the floor; end walls at 60° back to the drive wall. Safety nook ≤ every 100 m in a main travel way (WA regs), 1.5 m deep; a stockpile or intersection counts.

Fan stripping: 7 H (Site C 6.7 H, jumbo reach without benching). One twin 110 kW fan: 16.4 m incl. transition cuts; two in series: 24.6 m (Site A). Site D fan chamber: 9 m single / 18 m double plus 5 m transitions, both shoulders squared. Leave room for a 20 m vent bag between the fan and the next fillet.

## Intersections and fillets

- Intersection angle ≥ 70° preferred, 45° absolute minimum; sumps 55–60°. Avoid 4-way intersections: offset turnouts ≥ 13 m.
- Total span (drive + both fillets) ≤ 13 m (5 + 2 x 4). Excavations > 6 m wide need a geotech check / cable bolts.
- Fillet sizes (length along the wall): truck 3–4 m (Site A/Site B 4 x 4, Site C 3 x 3, Site D 3 m); large loader (R2900) 2–2.5 m; small loaders 1.5–2 m; no-access "standard" fillet 1 m (Site D, accounts for pillar rounding).
- Take a strip and one cut of the new drive, fully supported, before advancing the original heading past it (Site C).
- Never change from −1:7 to +1:7 without 10–15 m at ~1:50 between (rollover risk).
- Drive sizes and curve radii by equipment (Site B/Site C minimums): truck 5–5.5 W x 5–5.5 H, R 25 m; twin-boom jumbo 4–5 W, R 12–20 m; R2900/LH517/LH621 loader 4.5–5 W, R 12–20 m; 1700 loader 4 x 4, R 15 m.

Development orientation: cross major structures and contacts close to perpendicular; don't run capital development along or right next to ore contacts or regional structures; drives parallel to foliation squeeze (site-specific orientation wheel).

## Vertical development

- Method: long hole rise (LHR) for < 35 m with top and bottom access; raisebore for > 35 m; boxhole / Rhino (0.66–1.5 m, 750 mm at Site D, 5–30° off vertical) for blind slots > 20–25 m. ITH rig possible to 35 m (45 m with a raisebored slot).
- Vent rises: 25–35 m lifts, 5 m dia raisebore, or 4 x 5 / 4.5 x 6 m LHR (≈ 20–27 m²). Use a T or Y arrangement so the up-leg can be bogged if it chokes. ITH top site 7 W x 5.5 H x 14 L.
- Escapeway rise: 1.1–1.5 m dia, 75° ideal (55–80°); steel ladder ≥ 1.5 m, Safescape tube ≥ 1.1 m.
- Airleg rise: 1.2 x 1.2 m standard (max 1.5), 50–60°, ≤ 20 m long.
- Raises ≥ 3.5 m dia vertical; up to 20° off vertical is acceptable; never undercut existing development.
- LHR drill chamber: 3 m clear in front for the horseshoe, 2 m wider than the rise (~1.5 m carousel side). LHR sizes: 89 mm holes / 150 mm reamers → 2.4 x 2.0 m; 102 / 200 mm → 2.8 x 2.8 m. Karari: "star" LHR < 14 m, "slanted H" 14–22 m; boxhole rises 2.5 x 2.5 or 3 x 3.
- Raisebore: bottom access straight and flat ≥ 15 m, top and bottom on the same bearing; recirculation sump within 50 m of the collar sized 3 x pilot hole volume (sump length L = √(9πD²H / W) at −1:6).
- Electrical holes between substation cuddies: 3 x 200 mm + 5 x 150 mm (2 HV, 3 LV, comms, firing line, spare). Service holes > 40 m need a bigger diameter for cable clamps.

## Stopes

| Parameter | Typical value |
|---|---|
| Minimum width | 3.5–4 m (89 mm holes) |
| Maximum width | 20 m |
| Height, blind uphole | ≤ 20 m |
| Height, breakthrough | ≤ 40 m (level interval 25–40 m floor to floor) |
| Strike | 15 m (narrow / longitudinal) to 25 m (transverse, paste) |
| Section spacing | 3 m (Site A), 4–5 m (Site D) |
| Footwall angle | ≥ 50°, 55° preferred (ore won't run below 50°) |
| Floor | never down-dip; up to +1:20 |
| Back height change | < 1 m between sections |
| Crown chamfer | 40–50° against rock walls |
| Brow width | ≤ 6 m without geotech review (Site B) |

Shape rules: follow the economic ore boundary but keep it mineable and smooth; no wedges or bellies on the walls; avoid acute pillars; keep boundaries parallel / perpendicular to the drives so the rig sits square and brows are square. Sections perpendicular to the development at the stope ends. First ring ≥ 0.3 m inside the stope boundary; drill platform 1.5–2 m past the first ring. Flag any development within 15 m of a stope to geotech.

Brows and drawpoints: no brow inside an intersection; brows square to the drive; drawpoints not in paste. For remote bogging the loader must be straight before the brow: ≥ 19 m straight from the FWD to the final drawpoint (5 m rill + loader + 3 m). Paste walls 5–6 m from the final drawpoint, ≥ 3 m from intersections.

Drill and blast guide (Site A): burden 2.2–2.5 m / spacing 2.6–3.0 m for 89 mm (102 mm: 2.2–3.2 / 2.8–4.0); max hole 20 m (89 mm) / 35 m (102 mm); ring dump ≤ 30°; powder factor 0.35–0.45 kg/t rings, 0.7–1.0 slot; ~12 t per drill metre rings, 7.5 slot (Site E scheduling default 3.5 t/m overall); target void ratio 0.95. Each firing needs a free face and enough void; slot in the tallest / widest part. More D&B detail in [drill-and-blast-design.md](drill-and-blast-design.md); fill rules in [backfill-and-paste.md](backfill-and-paste.md).

Sequencing: paste-filled longitudinal top-down with a two-stope lead (45° front); rockfill longitudinal bottom-up with a two-stope lead; transverse paste stopes centre-out chevron with a 1–2 stope lead between levels.

Dilution and recovery (scheduling defaults, Site E): development 95 % recovery / 10 % unplanned dilution; stopes 90 % / 30 %. Site F uses ELOS (equivalent linear overbreak, from stability charts on hydraulic radius, dip and lift height) applied perpendicular to the hanging wall. Recovery applies to stope tonnes only.

## Services, safety and layout checks

- Two exits from every production level (decline + escapeway). Refuge chamber within 750 m walking of every face. Flag designs where egress / refuge / FAB is > 750 m.
- Passing bays every 120–150 vertical m on the decline: extend a turnout mouth by 9 m on a straight.
- Jumbo boxes every ~130 m (300 m max with two trailing cables).
- Dewatering: decline sumps ~30 m vertical apart with Flygt pumps; travelling mono ~100–130 m vertical; pump stations ~180–300 m vertical (depends on pump model). Drain holes collared from the top drive.
- Magazines connected to return air and away from both egress routes.
- Remote stockpiles ≤ 200–250 m from the stope.
- Breakthroughs: two HOLD lines ≥ 24 m apart on converging headings; pick up both, then one HOLD becomes a STOP. Don't break through at a turnout (fillets need a second firing). Breaking into a rise needs a risk assessment.
- Ventilation (WA regs, Site B manual): ≥ 0.05 m³/s per kW of diesel, ≥ 2.5 m³/s minimum in a working place; wet bulb > 25 °C needs ≥ 0.5 m/s air velocity, target < 28 °C, close the area at 30 °C, no work above 32 °C; DPM limit 0.071 mg/m³. Size vent rises ≥ 24–25 m² and keep the fresh / return separation in the design.
- Design changes / expiry (Site A): designs older than 6 months, new voids within 20 m, new holes within 10 m, pillars below 1:1, or a new breakthrough make it a high-risk change needing full review.
